This is Kate French's personal site. It collects only what you type into the contact form.

## What the site collects

- Your name, your email address and, if you add it, your company.
- Your answers to the form's questions and your message.
- The call time you picked, if you asked for a call.
- Which part of the site you came to the form from.

The site does not store your IP address or details about your browser. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

## How it is used

Kate reads what you send and replies to you. It is not sold, shared for marketing or added to a mailing list.

## Who else handles it

- **Cloudflare** hosts the site, stores the form entries and delivers the email that tells Kate about your message. Its spam check, Turnstile, runs on the contact form and sees your IP address and browser details in order to tell people from bots.
- **Google** provides Kate's inbox, so the email about your message arrives there. When a call is confirmed, the call goes on Kate's Google Calendar with your name, your email address and your message, and Google sends you the invitation and video link.

## How long it is kept

Form entries stay until Kate deletes them. Deleting an entry also removes any call request attached to it. Calendar events and email already sent stay in those accounts until they are deleted there.

## Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent, [send Kate a message](/contact) from the address you used, and she will take care of it.
